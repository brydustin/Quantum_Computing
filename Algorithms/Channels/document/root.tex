\title{Programs Are Channels\\[0.5em]
  \large loop-free QHLProver programs and the Kraus maps of the lectures}
\author{Dustin Bryant}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This document is generated by Isabelle from the theory \texttt{Program\_Channels} of the session
\texttt{HHL\_Algorithm\_Channels}.  It connects the two halves of the development: the programs
of the session \texttt{HHL\_Algorithm}, commands of the quantum while-language of the AFP entry \emph{QHLProver}, and the
completely positive maps of Paulsen's lectures.  For every loop-free program it reads off Kraus
operators from the program text, proves that the program's meaning is their Kraus map and that
they are complete, and concludes that the meaning is completely positive and trace preserving;
the HHL program is an instance.  The session is kept apart from the main document because it
loads both the notes and \texttt{HHL\_Algorithm}.  Proofs are folded.
\end{abstract}
