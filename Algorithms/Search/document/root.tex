This document is generated by Isabelle from the theory \texttt{HHL\_Search\_Bound} of the
session \texttt{HHL\_Algorithm\_Search}.  It supplies the oracle lower bound used in the
proof of Theorem~4(2) of Harrow, Hassidim and Lloyd: finding a zero of a random function with
$q$ quantum queries succeeds with probability at most $(5q+11)^2/N$.  The bound is imported
from the AFP entry \emph{Compressed Oracles}; the session is kept apart from the main document
because that entry builds on Complex\_Bounded\_Operators and Registers.  Proofs are folded.
